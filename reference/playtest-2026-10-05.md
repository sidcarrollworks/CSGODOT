## 1. Planting after the round ends

**Cause (verified).** `BombSystem` set `live = false` on `round_end`, and
`tick` refused a plant on a tick whose `round_end` was pending, so a plant
in progress was aborted and none could start in the round's end.

**CS2 (read, server.dll).** The plant (the C4's primary attack,
`180a18640`) asks only:
- whether this is retakes (`1809379f0`);
- whether the player is in a bomb zone (`pawn+0xf82`), or the plant-anywhere
  convar is on;
- whether they are on the ground (a hull trace).

It never asks whether the round is over. On completion it pays the planter
$300 (`180af9870(money, 3)` → `180af98b0`, `#Player_Cash_Award_Bomb_Planted`,
`cash_player_bomb_planted`), gated only on warmup being over
(`gamerules+0xd9`) and `mp_playercashawards`. The team awards and the loss
bonus are worked out inside TerminateRound (`18095e6f0` → `180955dd0`), so
a plant after the round adds the planter's $300 and nothing else.

**Fix.** `live` holds from `round_freeze_end` to the next `round_prestart`.
`Economy` pays the planter outside warmup only. Checks:
`run_bomb_system_checks.gd` (a plant spanning the round's end and one begun
after it both go down; the next round's prestart clears it) and
`run_economy_checks.gd` (+$300 to the planter, nobody else paid, the loss
ladder unchanged; nothing in warmup).

**Local.** In CS2 offline, kill every CT, plant in the round's end, and
confirm +$300 with no team line.

## 2. The molotov would not buy

**Cause (verified, reproduced headless at 1080p).** `UsePrompt._init`
overrode `HudElement._init` without calling `super()`, so the prompt kept
Godot's default `MOUSE_FILTER_STOP`. The prompt is a 1040 by 74 control
under the crosshair, and the HUD sits over the buy menu, as CS2's does. Its
text is emptied while the menu is open, but the control stayed, and it took
every click on the menu's fourth row:
- T: Tec-9, P90, SG 553, molotov;
- CT: Five-SeveN, P90, AUG, incendiary.

**Fix.** `super()` in `UsePrompt._init`. Checks:
- `run_hud_checks.gd`: every `HudElement` in `src/` lets the mouse through.
- `run_economy_checks.gd`: every card on both sides buys on a click through
  Godot's input, with the use prompt over the menu.

Both fail with the fix reverted.

**Found with it: the round's purchase limits were not CS2's (read,
CanAcquire `180ab2a20`).** All grenades shared one count, capped at 5, and
warmup never reset it. CS2 counts purchases by item, not by weapon type
(`mp_weapons_allow_typecount`, the services' `+0x298` counters keyed by
item definition), and outside warmup holds grenades to:
- **per kind:** no more than one can carry (two flashbangs, one of anything
  else; its return 3);
- **in all:** four grenades a round, net of sellbacks (`ammo_grenade_limit_total`;
  its return 0xf), a molotov and an incendiary counted as the larger of the
  two.

In warmup it skips the grenade limits and multiplies the item limit by
1000. The economy now does the same: `Economy._purchase_refusal`, with
`GRENADE_LIMIT` for the four, checked in `run_economy_checks.gd`. The
server's buy handler (`180a76230`) shows `#Cstrike_TitlesTXT_Cannot_Carry_Anymore`
for carrying refusals and nothing of its own for the round's limits; our
wording for those is our own.

## 3. G should throw the bomb (open)

**Cause (verified).** G drops the C4 when it is in hand
(`player_input.gd` → `BombSystem._on_drop`), but `C4._drop` puts it at the
feet, a static entity with no velocity.

**CS2 (read).**
- The drop command (`180b041a0`) drops the active weapon.
- The C4 goes through the same throw as a gun (`180aface0`): velocity
  `normalize(forward + (0, 0, 0.5)) × 250`, plus 0.6 of the player's
  velocity capped at 80. Where other weapons get speed 0, it keeps 250.
- Pickup waits 1.5 s for the dropper and 1.3 s for anyone else
  (`research/round-bomb-grenades.md` §1.6, §3.2); ours is 1.0.
- Our ItemDrops throws guns at 300, lift 0.25, plus the full velocity. That
  does not match the 250 literal: measure in CS2.

**Plan.**
- Drop the C4 as a physics `DroppedItem` thrown that way, with
  `bomb.position` following the entity while it is dropped.
- Pickups stay on the C4's own rules.
- The waits become 1.5 s and 1.3 s.
- A death throws it with the death velocity.

## 4. Spawning in the same spot

**Investigated (verified headless).** Within a match spawns change round to
round. A probe recorded where you spawn each round on dust2:

| Round | As T | As CT |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | (-808, -1141) | (2481, 258) |
| 2 | (-808, -1015) | (2353, 351) |
| 3 | (-843, -428) | (2370, 160) |
| 4 | (-756, -657) | (2370, 160) |
| 5 | (-756, -657) | (2439, 182) |

Repeats happen, as in CS2: CT fills dust2's five priority-0 points with five
players, all within about 200 units. But the shuffle was seeded by the
round alone (`hash([round_number, side, rounds_played])`), so every match
dealt the same sequence, round 1 always the same spot.

**CS2 (read).**
- RestartRound (`180951990`) sets `m_nSpawnPointsRandomSeed`
  (`gamerules+0xdb8`) once from `Plat_RelativeTicks` with
  `mp_randomspawn 0` ("Using spawn points configuration 0x%08X").
- Each round `18095b850` seeds `RandomSeed(totalRoundsPlayed ^ seed)`.
- It shuffles the points, stable-sorts them by priority, and shuffles the
  players.

**Fix.** `MatchState.spawn_seed`, drawn once a match from Godot's generator
(which a seeded check seeds), joins the round's seed. Checked in
`run_match_checks.gd`.

**Local.** If Sid still sees one spot every round of a match, log his spawn
per round in a real match: that is not what the probe shows.

## 5 and 9. Guns and dropped grenades hitting the ground (open)

**Cause (verified).**
- **No hook.** Nothing detects a dropped item's impact (`item_drops.gd`,
  `box3d_drops.gd`, `dropped_item_view.gd`).
- **Files not extracted.** The impact sounds were never extracted:
  `SOUND_FILTER` in `scripts/extract_assets.sh` lets in only the C4's beep
  and the incendiary's.
- **Surfaces blank.** Every item's `surface` in `weapons/physics.csv` is
  blank. The awk in `extract_assets.sh` stops at `m_pFeModel`, before
  `m_surfacePropertyHashes`.
- **No impact-speed volume.** `SoundEvent.gain()` has no impact-speed
  factor.

Thrown grenades' bounces are wired end to end. What Sid heard missing is
most likely the grenade a death drops, a dropped item.

**CS2.** The sound comes from the item's physics surface
(`surfaceproperties.vsurf`), for example `weapon.Rifle.Impact` and
`weapon.HEGrenade.Impact`:
- Physics mix group;
- silent from 1200 units;
- held off 0.2 s;
- volume scaled by impact speed.

server.dll has no impact-speed strings, so the sound is played outside the
server: a view.

**Plan.** An impact signal from the drop world, an `ItemSounds` view, the
impact-speed factor in `SoundEvent`, the surfaces read, and the files
extracted (Local).

## 6. The knife's pull-out sound

**Cause (verified).** `PlayerView` called `WeaponSounds.equip` only for an
entry with a `Weapon`, which only guns have, and the draws came from
`weapons/sounds.md`, guns only. The knife, the grenades, the bomb and the
Zeus (whose files name no draw) drew in silence.

**CS2 (read from the extracted clips).** Each first-person draw clip fires
client-only sound events at set times:
- the knife: `Weapon_Knife.Draw.Med` and `.Draw.Gear` at 0;
- the bomb: `Draw.Gear`, `c4.draw`, `c4.draw.beep` at 0.4 s and
  `c4.draw.grab` at 0.6 s;
- a grenade: `Draw.Gear` and its own draw, e.g. `HEGrenade.Draw` at 0.067 s;
- the Zeus: `Weapon_Taser.Draw` at 0.1 s.

Every one is in `sounds/sound_events.json`.

**Fix.**
- `scripts/weapon_tables.gd` writes `weapons/equipment_timings.csv`, the
  equipment's clips as `timings.csv` has the guns'.
- `WeaponSounds.draw` plays a draw clip's events through `SoundEvents`,
  each at its time, flat in the drawer's ears. A bot's is not heard.
- A new draw stops what is left of the last, as CS2's have since 21
  January 2026.
- Checked in `run_knife_checks.gd`.

## 7. The soccer ball (open)

**Cause (verified).** The ball is a `prop_physics_multiplayer` in dust2's
entities. The entry:
- model `dust_soccer_ball001`;
- at game (-368, 136.058, -1203.06);
- `spawnflags 1`: starts asleep.

It is drawn as part of the static world, and nothing collides with it.

**CS2 (read).**
- Players walk through pushaway entities. A think every 0.05 s
  (`1801e04a0`) shoves those within a pawn's grown box with
  `min(sv_pushaway_force / d, sv_pushaway_max_force)` (300000, 2000).
- Bullets push with the ammo type's impulse, e.g. 2400 for 7.62.
- Its shape is a sphere of radius 7.9 with surface `soccerball` (density
  900, elasticity 0.25).

**Plan.** A `PhysicsProp` sim entity simulated as a sphere in the drop
world, with CS2's pushaway and bullet impulses. Its mass is in the PHYS
block, not extracted yet (Local).

## 8. The jump

**Measured (float32 simulation of both integrators, 64 Hz).**
- **The standing jump matches CS2's:** 55.83 units.
- **The crouch jump:** we lift the feet 18 in the air where CS2 lifts 9
  (FinishDuck `180abdbe0`), 73.83 against 64.83. The camera holds high
  where CS2's drops 9 units over 0.2 s.
- **Jumping while ducked or ducking:** CS2 gives the full impulse
  (`180adf830`); ours takes the gravity adjustment off.
- **Jumping again right after a landing:** CS2 lowers it
  (`180ab21a0`), to full height only after 0.233 s.
- **Our camera dips** about 1 unit after takeoff and 1.6 at landing
  (`jump_camera_motion.gd`). CS2's ordinary view has no dip.

**Sid (2026-10-06):** the landing bounce is too much; halve it.

**Fixed (fix/jump-cs2-2026-10-06).**
- The airborne duck and unduck move the body 9, with CS2's duck root and
  view offsets eased by the eye update.
- The whole impulse ducked or ducking.
- CS2's scale for a jump soon after a landing, the landing timed within
  its movement interval.
- The landing bounce halved, 1.6 units of dip to 0.8.
- Bots space their jumps as CS2's CBot::Jump does (`1802ce3c0`). Without
  that, a bot short of a ledge jumped again on landing, lowered, and
  dust2's CT bots never made a 60-unit ledge on their way to Long.

Feet over the floor at 64 Hz (`tests/run_jump_height_checks.gd`):

| Jump | Peak |
|---|---|
| Standing | 55.83 |
| Crouch | 64.83 |
| Fully ducked | 57.00 |
| Again at once after a landing | 41.44 |

**Still open:** the crouch method's own duck timing on the ground
(`180abb2c0`, `sv_timebetweenducks`), the bhop window and jump-spam
penalty, under roadmap movement step 3. **Local:** a crouch jump and its
camera beside CS2.
