# Playtest, 2026-10-06: dust2 competitive

Sid played competitive dust2 on main at `d73aaf7` (#201 and #202 merged)
three times. The first two runs were in exclusive fullscreen at 3840x2160,
the second after changing NVIDIA settings. The third was under
`scripts/watch_game.gd`, 548 s, with every frame written to a file.
Four read-only investigations read the code, the extracted data and the
reference pages for each issue. Valve's leaked source was not used.
Every cause below is marked **verified** (read in the code or measured)
or **inferred**.

## 1 and 2. Holes on a dropped gun, and its sparks

**Cause (verified).** `BulletImpacts` and `HitEffects` set their decals'
`cull_mask` to everything but `RigModel.LAYER`. A dropped item's meshes
are on layer 1 with the world, so a hole in the floor beneath it was
painted on it too. Rounds already pass through dropped guns and push them
(`Box3DDrops.push_bullet_segment`), and nothing was drawn where they met.

**Fixed.**
- What lies or flies in the world and is not the world is drawn on its own
  layer, `DroppedItemView.LAYER` (1 << 18): items on the ground, the bomb's
  body, grenades. Holes and blood skip it, as CS2 paints them on the world.
- The push reports where it met each gun it pushed
  (`Hitscan.Result.items`). The views spark there, on a frame, with
  `impact_helmet_headshot`, outward along the gun's surface (Sid's choice).

**Local:** shoot a dropped gun beside CS2. Is it the same spark, and does
CS2 also play a sound?

## 3. Flickering near-white squares

Sid captured one on 2026-10-07: a light grey square with a white centre
where a round met a wall, about 17 pixels at 4K. Shooting a wall or the
ground made them reliably, and they showed around bodies. Overlapping
decals also swapped which was on top as the camera moved.

**Cause (verified)**, from contact sheets of each impact part drawn alone
on a sunlit dust2 wall, before and after:
- `impact_light_flash` (dirt, plaster, tile, wood, metal) has two
  renderers. CS2 draws the first additive, 50 times overbright, tinted
  231/205/182, at most 2% of the screen's height. The second is
  `particle_glow_04`, black at 40%, between 1.2% and 3%, its `ash_flecks`
  texture switched off. The table generator dropped the renderers' colour
  (`m_vecColorScale`), alpha scale (`m_flAlphaScale`), minimum size and the
  switched-off flag, and the draw ignored the size limits. So both drew
  `ash_flecks` white, at their size in the world, for 50 ms: the square
  was its frame at 50 times overbright, and its halo at full alpha.
- `impact_fx_hit_darken` is a dot tinted 33/27/27, between 0.5% and 0.8% of
  the screen, with no alpha nearer than about 76 units (a curve of the
  distance the spawn ignored). It drew white and large.
- Every impact mesh, the darken puff and concrete's and plaster's flecks,
  was drawn through a mirror image (`impact_basis`'s z is side x forward),
  so inside out and lit on its backs. The puff, opaque where its mask
  saturated, was a black star in the sun; flecks went black or white as
  they tumbled. Their colour was also drawn a channel at a time from a grey
  range: green, purple and red chips.
- Godot orders overlapping decals by their boxes' size and distance from
  the camera, so the newest hole was not always on top.

The investigation's other candidates (cut-out edges, occlusion culling's
jitter, glints on guns) were not what Sid captured and are unchanged.

**Fixed (fix/impact-artifacts-2026-10-07).**
- The generator keeps each renderer's colour (`color_scale`), alpha scale
  (`render_alpha`) and `min_screen`, and leaves out switched-off textures.
  Both draws apply them and hold a card between `min_screen` and
  `max_screen`, shares of the screen's height as the muzzle flashes read
  them. 39 renderers changed, the blood flash and the dark bursts on
  concrete and wood among them.
- A layer's `alpha_scale` (a scaled initial alpha) applies at the spawn.
- Meshes are drawn through the frame turned back. The puff is unlit
  (`csgo_effects` has no lighting inputs; inferred) with its texture's
  alpha. A fleck's colour is one blend of its two (inferred from the
  authored ranges), on the mean of CS2's fleck atlas, 107/100/98.
- Each new hole or blood mark sorts above every one before it
  (`BulletImpacts.next_decal_order`).

**Left as approximations:** sprites are unlit where CS2 lights them
(`m_flDiffuseAmount`), so dirt's and plaster's `fleks` chips are dark;
breakup textures (`tex_breakup`) and cards lying on the surface
(`ALIGN_TO_PARTICLE_NORMAL`) are not drawn; `fleks_baked`'s atlas, normal
and AO are not extracted yet.

**Local:** shoot walls, the ground and bots, near and far, beside CS2. Is
the light flash a quick spark, the darken a small dark dot at middle range,
the puff dark and soft?

## 4. Tearing and choppiness

**Read.** The game asks for V-Sync on (FIFO), capped at 224 on Sid's
240 Hz screen. FIFO cannot tear, so tearing would come from the driver
(inferred). In the watched run frames averaged 5.5 ms and the graphics
card alone took 4.5 ms of each at 4K, at the cap. Frames holding a tick
took 6.0 ms against 5.3 without one, and they come one, two or three
frames apart. Godot's delta smoothing can move a tick by a frame
(mechanism read in Godot's source; whether it engages here is not
confirmed). Godot has no NVIDIA Reflex. What Reflex gives is fewer frames
queued ahead; Godot's swapchain image count and frame queue are the
nearest settings.

**Plan:** delta smoothing off, fewer swapchain images, each measured in
alternating runs with the watcher; G-Sync confirmed with NVIDIA's
indicator.

## 5. The 90 fps seconds

**Measured** (`watch_game.gd`, 78,187 frames). Of the 2,388 frames of
8 ms or more, 60% had the frame's own scripts at 3 ms or more and 25% a
tick at 3 ms or more. Every second under 120 fps had 12 to 26 shots in
it, and its frames' scripts averaged 4 to 5 ms against 1.5. So what is
drawn or heard for shots costs most of it.

**Found.** `profile_combat.gd`'s new `split` stamps every node's frame
callback: in its fights HitEffects took 1.23 ms a frame where it takes
0.11 out of them, 1.02 ms of it the particles' draw. The draw works out
every live particle's place, size, fade, turn and frame from dictionaries,
about 18 us a particle in GDScript, and a burst into a wall keeps hundreds
alive (`profile_hits.gd`: 132 at 8 hits a second, 480 at 32).

**Fixed (perf/shooting-frames-2026-10-07).** The draw is native
(`HitParticleDraw`), the script kept as the reference and every check
holding the two to the same cards within float precision: 0.11 to 0.19 ms
at 8 hits a second where it was 2.61, 0.25 to 0.48 at 32 where it was
13.3 to 13.6. In the fights HitEffects now takes 0.49 ms a frame, its draw
0.13. What is left of it: the marks, the buffers' submission and the
spawns, about 0.1 ms each. **Local:** Sid's run with the watcher.

## 6. The running viewmodel

**Verified.** CS2's first-person animgraph has no walk or run clip and
no speed parameter (`reference/animgraph/viewmodel.md`, `parameters.md`),
and client.dll has no `cl_bob*` convars; it has `weapon_land_dip_amt`. Its
bob is in code (inferred: the viewmodel pose function the impact-tracer
research names). Ours (`view_model_motion.gd`) has a 1.46 s vertical and a
2.92 s lateral cycle with the AK, against steps 0.5 s apart, and little
real vertical movement.

**Plan:** read CS2's bob in Ghidra (client.dll: the 2026-10-04 project is
build 2000924, the installed one 2000927, so match the address first),
capture both side by side, then tie our bob to the steps.

## 7. The purchase sound

**Verified.** `BuyMenu` plays nothing, and a bought grenade is carried,
not drawn, so its draw does not play. CS2 plays
`UIPanorama.buymenu_purchase` to the buyer, and `Player.EquipArmor_<side>`
for those near when armour goes on (`research/audio-round.md`,
`audio-gameplay.md`). Neither file is in `SOUND_FILTER`.

**Plan:** a view that hears `item_purchase` and plays them, refusals
included; extract the files (Local).

## 8. Bots holding B

**Verified.** A CT bot's opening goal for B is the site's middle; on
arrival it stops and looks at the point before it on its own path, B
doors, about 120 degrees from upper tunnels, outside its 75-degree cone. It
never looks round while holding, nothing in `src/bots` hears, and it does
not listen for `hurt`. Holding until the plant is **not** explained: an
opening hold ends after 8 s.

**Plan:** face the enemy's approach and sweep while holding; turn when
shot; then hearing (footsteps decided on the tick, `player_footstep` sent,
noises kept on the world). **Local:** a logged replay of the B CTs.
